# rf-youtube-playlist-manager

## Privacy Policy - RF YouTube Playlist Manager

Last updated: February 5, 2026

### Information We Collect

This extension accesses your YouTube account through Google's OAuth 2.0 to perform playlist management actions. We collect:

- YouTube playlist data (video lists, playlist names)
- Your YouTube video watch progress (for sorting features)

### How We Use Information

- All data is processed locally in your browser
- We do not store, transmit, or share your data with any third parties
- API calls are made directly from your browser to YouTube's servers
- OAuth tokens are managed by Chrome's secure storage

### Data Retention

We do not retain any user data. All operations are real-time.
